# Clevertree LLC

**Software Projects**

Clevertree LLC builds embeddable open-source software — standing debates, investigation mesh, and music-as-code — hosted on Relay.

## How we build

Daily anonymous stats and tip review → next-day product patches. A small A-team plus LLM agents works at major-corp cadence. The edge is private, accurate visibility—not surveillance dossiers. North star: detailed user reports drive automated triage and patches without a developer in the middle.
